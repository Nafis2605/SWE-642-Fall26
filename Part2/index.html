About the Department

The Department of Computer Science at George Mason University is part of the College of Engineering and Computing and is located on the Fairfax, Virginia campus, just outside Washington, D.C. The department offers bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs, and serves a large and diverse student body that includes both full-time students and working professionals from the region's technology, government, and defense sectors.

Faculty in the department carry out research in areas such as artificial intelligence and machine learning, cybersecurity, software engineering, data science, computer vision, human-computer interaction, and visualization. Students get the chance to work alongside faculty in research labs, take evening classes that fit around a job, and build connections with the many companies and agencies in Northern Virginia.

Master of Science Degrees

The department offers the following MS degree programs:

  • MS in Computer Science
  • MS in Software Engineering
  • MS in Information Systems
  • MS in Data Analytics Engineering
